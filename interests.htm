My Interests & Hobbies

Sports Card Collecting

One of my favorite hobbies is collecting sports cards, especially basketball and football cards. I enjoy researching players, following their careers, and finding rare cards to add to my collection. I am particularly interested in young NBA players and watching how they develop throughout their careers. This hobby has also inspired me to explore opening my own sports card and collectibles shop.

Watching Sports

I enjoy watching professional basketball and football, especially the NBA and NFL. I like following my favorite teams, keeping up with player statistics, and watching younger athletes develop their skills. Watching sports is something I enjoy both for entertainment and because it connects to my interest in sports card collecting.

Family & Travel

Outside of school and sports, I enjoy spending time with my family and exploring new places. I especially enjoy planning family trips, visiting new destinations, and creating memories together. Traveling gives me an opportunity to experience different places and take a break from my everyday responsibilities.
